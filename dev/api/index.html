GameTracing.GameTracing — Module
GameTracing

Homotopy continuation for logit quantal response equilibria. trace_endpoint follows the branch of a logit game from zero precision (infinite temperature), where the only equilibrium is the flat one, up to a target precision with BifurcationKit's pseudo-arclength continuation (PALC), across folds. Failures are values (TraceFailure), never exceptions.

GameTracing.CorrectorSettings — Type
CorrectorSettings{E}(; reach, max_kl, logit_tolerance, pin_tolerance, contraction_limit,
                     contraction_offset, newton_iterations)

The corrector's controls (CorrectorReach); nothing switches a limit off. reach: a corrector that moves a logit further from the predictor than this times the predictor's own largest logit move is refused. shift_share: the share of that reach a redraw's predicted root shift may use (RootShift). max_kl: a corrected point whose largest per-logit probability KL from the predictor passes this is refused. A step's turn is limited by StepControl's turn_limit.

Convergence is scale-free, as in Gambit's tracer (path.cc:372, c_tol in log probabilities): a Newton correction that moves no logit more than logit_tolerance, at a relative precision within the relative pin_tolerance of its pin. A correction that moves the logits more than contraction_limit times the one before it (plus logit_tolerance times contraction_offset) fails to contract; BifurcationKit's step growth reads the same rate (corrector_quality). newton_iterations: Newton iterations per corrector (BifurcationKit's default, 25; Gambit's is 100).

GameTracing.LogitGame — Type
LogitGame(pass!, target_precision)

A logit game whose equilibria solve z = λ G(z, λ) at precision λ = 1 / temperature, G the payoff gains the logits scale. pass!(gain, gain_slope, gain_precision_slope, z, λ) writes G(z, λ), its Jacobian ∂G/∂z and its slope ∂G/∂λ into the three arrays, from one evaluation, and returns nothing. The arrays are views into the trace's own residual and Jacobian (BranchWorkspace), so a pass allocates nothing of its own. A pass that needs work arrays of its own gets them from pass_workspace. G must be finite at λ = 0, where the flat logits are the only equilibrium. target_precision is the precision a trace ends at.

GameTracing.NormalFormGains — Type
NormalFormGains(payoffs)

The pass! of a normal-form game with any number of strategies per player. payoffs[p][s] is player p's payoff at the pure profile s; every array has one type and one size, each player's strategy count along its own axis. Player p's logits are z_pk = log(π_pk / π_p1) for its strategies k ≥ 2, against its first strategy, stacked player by player (logit_ranges). Its gains are G_pk = U_p(k, π) - U_p(1, π), its expected payoffs against the rivals' mixed strategies, which do not depend on the precision. A pass writes into work arrays from pass_workspace; called without them, it builds its own.

GameTracing.StepControl — Type
StepControl{E}(; distance_limit, growth_limit, turn_limit)

BifurcationKit's CorrectorQuality thresholds, which shrink a step whose corrector looks poor. distance_limit is the Euclidean length of a Newton step's logit part (Gambit's 0.4 in log probabilities); growth_limit bounds a step's growth (Gambit's max_accel 1.1), and the step then shrinks as the distance or contraction nears its limit. turn_limit refuses a step whose tangent turns this many radians from the last one, Allgower and Georg's test (6.1.10) for a step across a fold onto another branch; 1 is under Gambit's quarter turn (t·newT < 0, path.cc). The corrector's contraction rate (CorrectorSettings) also slows growth. nothing switches a threshold off.

GameTracing.StepDistance — Type

The trace's step length in a state x = [w; ρ] of k payoff gaps w, at state_weight (the gaps' weight):

  • AveragedDistance: state_weight ‖Δw‖² / k + (1 - state_weight) Δρ².
  • SummedDistance: state_weight ‖Δw‖² + Δρ².
  • FisherDistance: state_weight Σ σ(zᵢ)σ(-zᵢ) Δwᵢ² + Δρ², with the Bernoulli Fisher weights of the logits z = ρ w.
  • FisherLogPrecisionDistance: state_weight Σ σ(zᵢ)σ(-zᵢ) (Δwᵢ / P)² + (Δλ / (1 + λ))² at the precision λ = ρ P, P the target: Fisher's payoff gains w / P beside the step in log(1 + λ) to first order, LogitNash's path variable. Neither term depends on P, so the target only caps the trace.

Both Fisher distances freeze their weights at each accepted point. The corrector's reach check measures in logits under every distance (within_reach).

GameTracing.TraceSettings — Type
TraceSettings{E}(; first_step, max_step, aggressiveness, geometry, first_tangent_divisor,
                 corrector, max_steps, tangent, bordered_solver, step_control, orientation_check, palc_corrector,
                 palc_options, continuation_options)

BifurcationKit PALC controls for trace_endpoint, in (w, ρ) with w = logits / ρ the payoff gaps and ρ the relative precision. Step sizes are arclength in geometry's norm (StepDistance); the default is the Fisher distance (TraceGeometry).

  • first_step, max_step: first and largest arclength step. A step whose corrector fails halves; the trace ends as TraceStuck once its predictor moves the point no more than a few ulps of its size (step_unresolved). BifurcationKit's dsmin is zero: its halving stops at a zero step. After the predictor stops moving, BifurcationKit still halves to zero through the subnormals, about 150 halvings of one refused residual evaluation each.
  • aggressiveness (BifurcationKit's a): without a step_control growth limit, a step grows by 1 + a ((N - n) / N)² after a corrector of n of its N = corrector.newton_iterations iterations.
  • corrector: a CorrectorSettings.
  • geometry: a TraceGeometry, the norm a trace steps in and its state weight. first_tangent_divisor: the first tangent is the secant to a solve at first_step / first_tangent_divisor.
  • max_steps: continuation steps before the trace fails as NoConvergence.
  • tangent: BifurcationKit's tangent algorithm, passed to PALC as is. Bordered() (the default) solves the bordered Jacobian system. Secant() extrapolates the last two points and saves that solve. Do not use it where a small payoff perturbation unfolds a pitchfork: there it crosses onto another arm (the test "a pitchfork unfolded by a payoff perturbation"). Over 40 random unfoldings at payoff scale 1e-4 it kept the branch in none, Bordered() in all 40.
  • bordered_solver: a BorderedSolver.
  • step_control: a StepControl, or nothing for none.
  • orientation_check orients a bordered tangent by the determinant of the bordered matrix. It takes effect only with MatrixSolve, the one solver that has a determinant.
  • palc_corrector: PALC's corrector keyword, nothing by default, which is BifurcationKit's own Newton method (newton_palc; it stops on leaving [0, 1]). BifurcationKit.FreeParameterNewton() leaves ρ free there instead, the trace's choice before this field. CorrectorReach reads any corrector's Newton callbacks.
  • palc_options: a NamedTuple of further keywords for BifurcationKit.PALC, which override the trace's own (tangent, θ, dotθ, bls, step_control, orientation_check, corrector, orientation_dot). Do not override step_control, orientation_check, orientation_dot, θ, dotθ or bls: the trace's step shrinking, tangent orientation and geometry rest on them, and TraceSettings fields set them consistently.
  • continuation_options: a NamedTuple of further keywords for BifurcationKit.ContinuationPar, which override the trace's own. Do not override newton_options, p_min, p_max, ds or the dsmin/dsmax pair: CorrectorReach converges a corrector on its own test and needs tol = 0, and the trace ends at p_max.

GameTracing.trace_endpoint — Method
trace_endpoint(game, width, settings; keep_going, accept_corrected, redraw, context)

Follow game's branch of width logits from the flat start (zero logits, the only root at precision zero) to game.target_precision, and return Ok(Traced) logits there or Err(TraceFailure) for the failure that stopped short (a TraceResult). The relative precision ρ is the precision in units of the target.

keep_going(logits, ρ) runs at each accepted point before the next step's corrector, and false stops the trace. accept_corrected(logits, ρ, converged) runs as each corrector ends, and false refuses a converged point, which halves the step. A trace that stops short fails with NoConvergence when it spends settings.max_steps and TraceStuck otherwise (a step below the float type's resolution, an unchanged accepted point, or a refusal); a non-finite start or a broken linear solve fails NotFinite or Singular. context rides on every failure.

redraw(logits, ρ, shift) runs at each accepted point short of ρ = 1 that keep_going passes, and returns nothing, a new game of the same type and target precision, or the ShiftedGame that shift(game) (RootShift) returned for one. That check tells whether a candidate game's root lies within the corrector's reach, so a caller can refine a draw that does not before it returns; the restart reuses its factorization of the new game's Jacobian. A new game replaces the map for the steps after that point: the trace restarts there along the new map's tangent, oriented by the old one, with the step it held, and its next corrector lands on the new map under the same step control. The endpoint, with no step after it, is never redrawn.

GameTracing.CorrectorReach — Type
CorrectorReach(accept_corrected, settings, geometry, previous, predicted, step_length)

The trace's BifurcationKit callback_newton, which also decides the corrector's convergence: a settled iterate ends Newton's method converged (NewtonConverged), and a correction that fails to contract stops it (corrected!). Two such corrections in a row, each larger than the predictor's own move in logits, are suspected rounding noise (noise), confirmed at the next halved step; the check then refuses every corrector (noise_confirmed). A non-finite residual stops the corrector, and so does any iterate whose logits are further from the predictor's than settings.reach times the predictor's own largest logit move (within_reach); the step then halves. A predictor within RESOLUTION_ULPS ulps of each of the point's coordinates (step_unresolved) refuses that corrector and every later one (unresolved): no smaller step can add a point, so the trace ends as TraceStuck, or as PrecisionExhausted when the corrector before it stopped past its reach (unresolved_noise). On a smooth branch that distance is the branch's curvature times ds² / 2, so halving brings any step under it, while an iterate that lands on another branch is far from it. A converged closing point is refused the same way past reach, past a largest probability KL KL(corrected ‖ predicted) of settings.max_kl (largest_kl). A predictor that did not move has no reach to compare. accept_corrected(logits, ρ, converged) then sees every other closing point. previous and predicted hold the current step's start and predictor, iterate the current solve's last iterate, all updated in place; state (CorrectorState) holds the scalars that change between calls.

GameTracing.RootShift — Type
RootShift(point, border, limit)
RootShift(point, step, border, settings)

The check a redraw hook receives at the accepted point point = [w; ρ]. Called on a candidate game, it returns the ShiftedGame: one evaluation of the game's map and Jacobian at point, the bordered system under the corrector's border factored once, and the root shift δ from [∂F/∂x; borderᵀ] δ = [-F(point); 0], one Newton step of the bordered corrector. The old map's residual at an accepted point is within the corrector's tolerance, so -F(point) stands for F_old(point) - F_new(point). The shift is within when it moves no logit further than limit: settings.shift_share of the corrector's reach on the next step, the predictor step (CorrectorSettings), or nothing when the reach is off, and every game passes. A hook returns the ShiftedGame to install its game, and the restart reuses its factorization.

GameTracing.TraceFinalise — Type
TraceFinalise(keep_going, redraw, slot, settings)

BifurcationKit's finalise_solution of one trace segment. An accepted point bitwise equal to the branch's last one (a step below the point's rounding) stops the trace, a stall, as does a point that closes a loop of the branch (looped). The trace goes on while keep_going(logits, relative_precision) holds. Then redraw(logits, relative_precision, shift) may return a new game, where shift is the point's RootShift check under settings: slot, a Ref to the current RedrawSlot (the constructor wraps the one it is given), takes it and the segment stops, for trace_endpoint to correct the point onto it and restart. A redraw that returns nothing or the current game swaps nothing. JULIA_DEBUG=trace lists every point.

check's verdict on a Newton iterate that its other tests let proceed, in logits z = ρ w. The first iterate is the solve's start and never settles. Each later one is a correction Δz from the iterate before: it settles (NewtonConverged) when settled. A correction past contraction_limit times the one before (plus logit_tolerance · contraction_offset) fails to contract, Gambit's test (path.cc), and stops the corrector, so the step halves. Two in a row, each larger than the predictor's own move, are suspected noise (noise); the step still halves. A second such solve before any settles, its correction at least half the suspect's, confirms it (noise_confirmed): halving shrinks the corrections of a predictor past a fold, not noise. Every later solve refuses.

GameTracing.projection_step — Method
projection_step(game, logits, relative_precision, geometry)

One minimum-norm Gauss–Newton step from x = [logits / ρ; ρ] onto game's branch F(x) = 0 in payoff gaps, in geometry's plain norm W (coordinate_weight, level_weight): dx = -W⁻¹ Jᵀ (J W⁻¹ Jᵀ)⁻¹ F. It is the minimum-norm solution of (J W^-½) y = -F by pivoted QR, scaled back by W^-½; the normal matrix is never formed. The step moves ρ as well, so it stays defined at a fold, where a correction at fixed ρ is singular. Payoff gaps w = z / ρ exist at every finite ρ ≠ 0, so ρ must be one.
